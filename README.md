PennyWise - Personal Money Tracker App

Tagline: A personal finance tracker for Filipinos, covering everyday income and expenses plus a built-in utang (debt) and bills ledger called ListaPay.

Overview

PennyWise helps you see where your money goes and who owes you what. The Dashboard summarizes your total balance, monthly income and expenses, budget progress, and spending by category. Transactions, Accounts, and ListaPay each have their own page.

Key features

Accounts: Cash, bank, e-wallet, and card accounts with balances derived from transactions. Deleting an account requires moving its history elsewhere, so no money is created or lost.
Transactions: Income, expense, and transfer records with search and filters by type, account, and category.
ListaPay: A ledger for people who owe you, group debts with per-member amounts, and bills you need to pay.
Partial and full payments, overdue tracking, and due-soon notifications.
Lending and repayments create linked transactions that stay in sync with the debt, so the two can’t drift apart.
Budgeting: A monthly budget with a progress meter.
Authentication and cloud sync: Email/password and Google sign-in through Supabase, with per-user data protected by row-level security.
Responsive design: A sidebar on desktop and a bottom navigation bar on mobile.
